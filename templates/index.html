FAQ

Good to know

Why can't I fetch some posts?

Only publicly available media can be fetched. Private accounts, sign-in-only posts and anything the platform blocks may be unavailable, and platforms change over time.

Do you keep my files or links?

No. Post details are held for 15 minutes so your download links work, and files are written temporarily while being prepared and then deleted. See the privacy policy.

Is it free? Are there limits?

It is a free beta. To keep it fast for everyone there are request limits, up to 250 MB per file, and 500 MB per ZIP.

Can I download anyone's content?

Only save content you own or have permission to use. See the terms and the copyright and takedown page.
